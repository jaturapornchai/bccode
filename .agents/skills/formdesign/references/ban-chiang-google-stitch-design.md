- Warm earth-tone terracotta accents.

- Compact data-first layout.
- 1px structural borders.
- Soft terracotta-tinted shadows.
- Active navigation should be clear and strong.
- Tables, filters, search bars, forms, and action rows should be dense and easy to scan.
- Use low-opacity decorative motifs only when they do not reduce readability.
