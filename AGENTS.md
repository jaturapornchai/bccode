A PostgreSQL/ClickHouse row may carry the source record's `guidfixed` for traceability, but joins and references still use business keys. **Rationale (reinforced 2026-07-22 by Jead)**: `guidfixed` is a per-database identity for confirming/CRUD-ing records inside MongoDB only — it is **not portable**: export/import, seed rebuilds, and server migrations do not carry it over, so any relation keyed on `guidfixed` silently breaks after a move, while relations keyed on business codes re-link cleanly. This applies to data-model diagrams (mongomodel) too: never draw a relation from/to `guidfixed`, and every referenced master must expose a business `code` (e.g. `holding.code` is what `holdingcode` references).
- **Business Reference Code Rule**: Business reference fields between records must store the relevant user/business code, never the target record GUID. Example: product or barcode unit references store `unitcode`, not product-unit `guidfixed`. A referenced master that can participate in relations must have a non-empty unique business key; if `code` is optional, require it before linking or define another explicit business key instead of falling back to GUID.
- **Company-Owned Operational Data Rule (set 2026-08-05 by Jead; supersedes Holding-Shared Master Rule)**: A Holding is the parent permission and consolidation boundary, not the owner of mutable business masters. Product, barcode, unit, product classification, warehouse, customer, debtor, creditor, related groups/settings, documents, transactions, events, cache rows, and projections belong to exactly one `(holdingcode, businesscode)`. Resolve `businesscode` from server-validated workspace context, verify the company and user scope, and never trust a mutation-body actor/company value. Holding-wide endpoints are read-only aggregation and must retain `businesscode` on every row/group so equal codes from different companies never merge. Holding-owned records are limited to Holding identity/membership, permissions/approvals, and consolidation configuration; universal platform reference dictionaries may remain global.
- **Product/Barcode Business-Key Rule (updated 2026-08-07 by Jead)**: Product `code` is unique by `(holdingcode, businesscode, code)`. An active Barcode is unique by `(holdingcode,businesscode,barcode)` for deterministic scanning; its required `itemcode` links a Product in the same company and `itemunitcode` must match the Product base unit or one of its `unitconversions[]`. Quick Barcode creation must atomically create or link a minimal Product—unlinked Barcode records are invalid. Barcode persists scan identity, `itemcode`, `itemunitcode`, main-barcode status, exact decimal prices, and optional Barcode-owned `imageuri`/`images`/`videos`/`description`; Product owns every unit ratio. Product may aggregate same-company Barcode media and unit matches at read time, but `barcodes` is virtual (`bson:"-"`) and must never be copied into Product storage or Product Kafka events.
- **Business Code Uppercase Rule**: User-facing business/reference/display code values must be normalized to uppercase before validation, duplicate checks, search, save, and downstream sync. This covers fields such as `code`, `<thing>code`, `businesscode`, `businesscodes[]`, company code, product code, unit code, document code, SKU, and equivalent business codes. Do not apply this to persisted/API field names (lowercase, underscore allowed), `holdingcode` (must stay lowercase), `langcode`, email/user login text, or GUID values.
- **Business Code No-Space Rule** (set 2026-06-21 by Jead): รหัส (business/reference/display code) ห้ามมีช่องว่าง — ต้องตัด whitespace ทุกชนิด (space, tab, newline) ทั้งหัวท้ายและภายในออกก่อน validation, duplicate check, search, save, และ sync เช่น `qatest perm 01` → `QATESTPERM01` (ใช้คู่กับ uppercase rule ด้านบน). field scope และข้อยกเว้นเหมือน uppercase rule (ครอบคลุม `permissioncode`, `groupcode`, `productcode`, `unitcode`, document code, SKU ฯลฯ; ยกเว้น `holdingcode`, `langcode`, email/login text, GUID, และชื่อ field แบบ lowercase). Backend ตัด/ปฏิเสธรหัสที่ยังมีช่องว่างก่อนบันทึกเสมอ (ห้ามเชื่อ frontend อย่างเดียว).
- **Product Unit Ownership Model**: Product units belong to exactly one `(holdingcode, businesscode)` and are unique by normalized `unitcode` inside that company. Do not store `businesscodes[]` or `companyguids[]` availability selectors on units or other company-owned masters. Products, barcodes, documents, and projections reference units by `(businesscode, unitcode)`; `guidfixed` remains CRUD/audit identity only.
- **Product Base And Additional Unit Rule**: Every Product persists exactly one top-level standard unit identified by non-empty `unitcode` (with its `unitnames` snapshot when available), `dividevalue=1`, `standvalue=1`, and `condition=false`. Additional units live only in `unitconversions[]` as `{unitcode,unitnames,dividevalue,standvalue}` with unique normalized codes and exact positive integer ratios; the array never stores Barcode identity. Match linked Barcodes read-only by `productbarcode.itemunitcode == unitcode`. The Product editor may replace the base unit but cannot clear it or remove an additional unit still used by a Barcode. Enforce this in backend Create/Update and direct Product creation paths such as Quick Barcode. When a Product unit name or ratio changes, atomically refresh every linked Barcode unit snapshot and republish its PostgreSQL projection; sale/purchase/stock paths must receive the Product-owned ratio and must not silently fall back to `1:1`. `refbarcodes[]` is not a unit model.
- **Product Stock, Cost, Marketplace Stock, and Dimension Price Rule**: Accounting stock belongs to company-scoped product stock/ledger records and supports product, warehouse, and storage-location balances. Costing uses accounting stock only: normally one cost per product; with `costbywarehouse`, calculate per warehouse then aggregate to product. Marketplace dimension availability remains a separate company-scoped channel projection and is never accounting stock/cost. Selling prices may exist at product, barcode/unit, price-level, marketplace, and detailed dimension levels using exact decimal values. - **Mobile/Computer Product Operations Rule**: Mobile phone, SIM, computer, and serialized electronics workflows must expose structured screens for `ทะเบียนเลขเครื่อง` and `ราคาตามช่องทางขาย`. Channel price records track product/barcode/dimension, sales channel, price level, currency, sale price, compare-at price, effective dates, sync flag, and company access. Do not hide these workflows as raw JSON fields.
- **Product List Datalist PostgreSQL Rule**: Product master datalists read the company-scoped PostgreSQL `product` projection, then hydrate MongoDB detail by `(businesscode, code)`. Every Barcode already links a Product. Product lists may show product/accounting balance; Barcode lists must not show a balance. Projection queries must use source-verified columns and include `businesscode` in filters/joins/keys.

CRUD handlers write MongoDB first, then publish a Kafka event or durable MongoDB outbox event. PostgreSQL consumers build rebuildable relational projections from those events, and ClickHouse consumes processed facts/projections for BI. CRUD handlers must not write PostgreSQL or ClickHouse directly unless the task is explicitly a rebuild/sync/projection/BI worker.

ClickHouse is the BI/analytics/reporting store fed from processed facts/projections.
